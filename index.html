How paying works here

Payments happen in your UPI app. Split only records them — a payment counts as settled only after the receiver confirms it.

Tap Pay — Step 1: the payer marks paid (UPI ID and payment app are optional; the UTR / transaction ID is required when a UPI app is selected, and stays optional only for Cash or Bank transfer). The payment goes to Pending and the debt is not cleared yet. Step 2: the receiver checks their UPI app and taps Confirm received — only then balances update and a receipt is generated (Payment ID, from, to, amount, UPI ID, UTR, app, marked time and confirmation time). While pending, the payer can cancel. After confirmation, Undo is on the receipt and in Activity. Split never moves money and never verifies a bank transfer itself — confirmation is the receiver saying they received it.
